How we work

The rules we hold ourselves to, learned the hard way.

From scratch

Every Loom starts from random weights. We never fine-tune someone else's model.

Tests first

We write the tests and the pass marks before training, and keep them out of the training data.

Report the misses

When a model misses its bar, we say so on its card instead of moving the bar.

Write it down

Every run, number and mistake goes into the lab notebook, so nothing is learned twice.
